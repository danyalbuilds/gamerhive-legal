Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

GamerHive ("we", "us") is a mobile app that helps gamers find teammates by game and skill rank. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have over it.

Information we collect

Account information: your email address and password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your raw password).

Profile information you provide: username, avatar photo, bio, region, spoken language, the games and ranks you add, player tags, and your weekly availability.

Activity data: your online/offline status, invites you send or receive, chat messages (including voice notes) with other users, friend connections, party membership, ratings you give or receive, and any reports or blocks you file.

We do not collect your precise location — "region" is a label you choose yourself, not GPS data. We do not currently use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

How we use it

  • To operate the app — matching you with teammates, running chat and voice notes, sending in-app and push notifications you've opted into.
  • To keep the community safe — enforcing blocks, reviewing reports.
  • To improve the app based on how it's used.

Sharing

We don't sell your data. Your data is processed by our backend provider, Supabase, which stores and secures it on our behalf. We don't share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Chat messages are automatically deleted after 30 days. Your profile and account data is kept until you delete your account, at which point it — and your chats — are removed. You can delete your account at any time from Settings.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove most profile information at any time from the app.
  • Set your profile to "matched only" visibility in Settings to limit who can see it.
  • Block or report another user directly from their profile or a chat.
  • Delete your account and data at any time from Settings → Delete Account.
  • Contact us (below) to ask what data we hold about you or to request its deletion.

Children's privacy

GamerHive is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Security

We use industry-standard practices (encryption in transit and at rest, row-level access controls) to protect your data, but no service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact
